01Website conversion · Delhi NCR rooftop solar

Solarxp: a website that starts conversations.

A brand-new rooftop solar company needed to look established from day one, and needed every visitor to become a real conversation.

The challenge

Solarxp launched in 2026 into a crowded Delhi NCR market. Rooftop solar is a large investment, so homeowners judge a company on how professional it looks online. Solarxp had no website, an old logo, and no way to capture interest from people who found them.

What we did

  • Refreshed the brand with a new logo built around a solar panel and a globe.
  • Designed a fast, mobile-first website that explains rooftop solar, savings and the installation process in plain English.
  • Made WhatsApp the main call to action. Every form submission lands straight in the business WhatsApp, so no enquiry waits.
  • Added click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons on every screen, because most solar buyers browse on their phones.
  • Set up hosting with automatic updates from GitHub, so changes go live in minutes.

The story

When we met Solarxp, the brand existed only in the founder's head and on a WhatsApp number. Buyers who heard about them had nowhere to check them out.

We started with trust: a clean identity and a website that answers a homeowner's real questions before they ask. Then we removed every step between interest and conversation. Today, a visitor can go from reading about rooftop solar to chatting with Solarxp in one tap.

02Local SEO · Google reviews

Sungrid: turning happy customers into Google reviews.

Sungrid did great installations, but Google didn't show it. We built a system that turns every finished project into public proof.

The challenge

Before calling a solar company, most homeowners search on Google and check the star rating. Sungrid had satisfied customers, but very few of them had left a review. Competitors with more reviews looked safer, even when Sungrid's work was better.

What we did

  • Fully optimised their Google Business Profile: the right categories, every service listed, real installation photos and regular updates.
  • Built a simple review request flow. After each installation, the customer gets a friendly WhatsApp message with a direct link to leave a review.
  • Gave the installation team review cards with a QR code to hand over on handover day, when customers are happiest.
  • Replied to every review, so new buyers can see a company that listens.
  • Improved local SEO with location and service pages targeting the areas Sungrid serves.

The story

Sungrid's problem wasn't quality, it was visibility. Their best salespeople were their past customers, but nobody was asking those customers to speak up.

We made asking automatic and effortless. The review request arrives at the right moment, takes one tap, and the team never has to remember to send it. Review by review, Sungrid's Google profile started to reflect the work they'd been doing all along, and the trust gap with competitors closed.

03Video production · Social media

Skynxt Solar: making solar easy to understand.

Solar is hard to explain in a post. So we showed it: short, clear videos and a social presence people actually stop for.

The challenge

Homeowners have the same questions about solar: what it costs, how much it saves, how long installation takes and whether it's worth it. Skynxt Solar posted occasionally, without a plan, and their content didn't answer those questions or build recognition.

What we did

  • Planned a monthly content calendar built around buyers' real questions.
  • Produced short vertical videos: installation timelapses, site walkthroughs, and simple savings explainers.
  • Created branded templates so every post is instantly recognisable as Skynxt Solar.
  • Posted consistently on Instagram and Facebook Reels, and reused the best videos as ad creatives.
  • Added clear calls to action, so viewers can send an enquiry straight from a post.

The story

Skynxt Solar didn't need more posts, they needed the right posts. We started by listing every question a homeowner asks before going solar, then turned each answer into a short video.

Real rooftops, real installations, real explanations. The result is a feed that does the selling before the sales call: by the time someone messages Skynxt Solar, they already understand what they're buying and trust who they're buying from.

04Lead generation · Meta + Google ads

RK Solar: solar leads at ₹60–80 each.

RK Solar needed a steady flow of enquiries at a cost that made every installation profitable.

The challenge

Solar leads can get expensive fast, and many are low quality: renters, people outside the service area, or people just browsing. RK Solar needed enquiries from real homeowners in their area, at a predictable cost.

What we did

  • Targeted homeowners in RK Solar's service areas on Facebook, Instagram and Google.
  • Wrote ads around what buyers care about most: lower electricity bills, government subsidy and a trusted local installer.
  • Added qualifying questions to lead forms, such as property type and monthly electricity bill, to filter out poor-fit enquiries.
  • Set up instant WhatsApp follow-up so every lead hears from RK Solar within minutes.
  • Tested creatives every week, cutting what didn't work and putting budget behind what did.

The story

RK Solar had tried ads before and paid for a lot of enquiries that went nowhere. The issue wasn't the ads alone, it was everything around them: who saw them, what they promised, and how fast anyone followed up.

We rebuilt the whole path, from targeting to the first WhatsApp message. With better-fit audiences, clearer ads and qualifying questions, enquiries came in at ₹60–80 per lead, and the sales team spent its time on homeowners who were actually ready to talk.
